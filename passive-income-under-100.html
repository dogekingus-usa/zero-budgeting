Passive Income Ideas Under $100: Start Earning While You Sleep

The biggest myth about passive income is that you need capital to start. While having money certainly helps, there are legitimate ways to build income streams for less than $100 — some for free. Here are five proven ideas you can start this weekend.

1. Digital Products (Cost: $0-50)

Create a PDF guide, template, or workbook once and sell it forever. Platforms like Stripe, Etsy, or Payhip handle hosting and payments. A simple budgeting spreadsheet or resume template costs nothing to create (Canva is free) and can generate hundreds in sales over time. The key: solve a specific problem for a specific audience.

2. Print on Demand (Cost: $0)

Design t-shirts, mugs, or phone cases using free tools like Canva. Upload designs to Printful or Printify connected to an Etsy or Shopify store. They print and ship on demand — you never hold inventory. Even selling 5 items a month at $10 profit each covers your cell phone bill.

3. Affiliate Marketing (Cost: $0-30 for domain)

Write about products you actually use and include affiliate links. Amazon Associates, ShareASale, and CJ Affiliate are free to join. Start with a simple blog or social media account focused on a niche: "Budget Home Office Setup" or "Best Gear for Dog Owners." Useful content + honest recommendations = passive commissions.

4. Create a Simple Course (Cost: $0-100)

Platforms like Skillshare and Udemy let you publish courses for free. Film on your phone, edit with free software (DaVinci Resolve or CapCut), and teach something you know: how to meal prep on $50/week, how to use Excel, how to start a garden. One good course can earn for years.

5. License Your Photos or Music (Cost: $0)

If you take decent photos or create music, upload to stock sites like Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, or AudioJungle. Each download pays a small royalty, but over time these add up. One viral photo can earn hundreds of dollars monthly for years.

Pro Tip: Don't Chase Trends

The best passive income streams solve problems that don't change. Budgeting, organization, cooking, parenting, fitness — evergreen niches. Build something once that helps people, and the income follows.
