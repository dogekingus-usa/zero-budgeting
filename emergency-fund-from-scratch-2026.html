Step 1: The Zero-Based Budget Audit (Week 1)

Zero-based budgeting means every dollar of income is assigned a purpose âEUR" bills, savings, expenses, or fun money. Nothing is left unallocated. Here's how to set it up for emergency fund building:

1 Track Every Dollar for 7 Days

Write down every single purchase, bill, and transfer for one week. Use a notes app, a spreadsheet, or paper. Don't judge âEUR" just track. You're looking for patterns, not perfection.

2 Categorize Your Spending

Group your expenses into four buckets: Fixed Essentials (rent, utilities, insurance), Variable Essentials (groceries, gas, medicine), Discretionary (dining out, streaming, shopping), and Waste (late fees, unused subscriptions, convenience markups).

3 Find Your First $200 in Monthly Savings

Average households leak $300-$600 per month in waste and unnecessary spending. Audit these common categories:

  • Subscriptions âEUR" Average household spends $55/month on unused subscriptions
  • Dining out âEUR" One fewer takeout meal per week saves $40-$60/month
  • Coffee/Drinks âEUR" $40-$80/month on daily coffee runs
  • Convenience spending âEUR" $30-$50/month on marked-up convenience store items
  • Bank fees âEUR" Switch to a no-fee bank and save $10-$35/month
