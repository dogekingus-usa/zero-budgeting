Financial Independence on Any Income

Financial independence isn't about how much you make âEUR" it's about how much you keep and grow. Zero-based budgeting gives you the control and visibility needed to build wealth systematically.

The FI Number

Your FI number = Annual expenses -- 25 (for the 4% rule). If you spend $40,000/year, you need $1,000,000 invested. Zero-based budgeting helps you optimize both sides: reduce expenses and increase savings.

Phase 1: The Foundation (Months 1-6)

Month 1: Start zero-based budgeting. Track every dollar.
Month 2: Cut expenses by 10%. Reduce subscriptions, dining out, and impulse purchases.
Month 3: Build your $1,000 starter emergency fund.
Month 4-6: Increase savings rate to 20% of income.

Phase 2: Acceleration (Months 7-18)

Push savings rate to 30-50%. Every dollar saved is a dollar that can work for you. Invest in low-cost index funds (VTI, VXUS, BND). Automate everything.

Phase 3: Optimization (Year 2+)

Increase income through side hustles, skills development, and career advancement. Every additional dollar should be 80-90% saved (avoid lifestyle inflation).

The Math That Matters

At a 50% savings rate, you reach FI in ~17 years. At 70%, it's ~8 years. Zero-based budgeting is the tool that makes these savings rates possible.
