Set Clear Goals

The first step to paying off student loans faster is setting realistic goals. Here are two popular methods:

  • Debt Snowball Method: Focus on paying off the smallest balances first, using the extra cash from smaller payments to pay down larger ones more quickly. This method can give you a psychological boost early on.

Remember, the more precise your budget, the easier it will be to stick to it and meet those debt repayment goals.
