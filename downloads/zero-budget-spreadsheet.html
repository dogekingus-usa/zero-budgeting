Zero-Budget Spreadsheet — Your Money Map

Welcome to ZeroBudgeting. This template gives every dollar a job. Plug in this month's numbers and see exactly where your money goes.

How to use this template

  1. Copy the template — in Google Sheets: File → Make a copy. In Excel: Save As → your own file.
  2. Fill in your income — all sources, take-home amounts only.
  3. Assign every dollar — categories below, until income minus expenses = $0.
  4. Track daily — update as you spend. The tracker column is your reality check.

The 15-minute setup

1) Copy this page's table into a new sheet. 2) Fill income + fixed bills. 3) Assign the rest. 4) Check in every Sunday. That's it — the most important financial habit you'll ever build.

Tip: the built-in tutorial tab analogy — start with just 3 categories if full tracking feels heavy. Perfect beats paralysis.
